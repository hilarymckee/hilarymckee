Who I work with

I work with adults who want to understand themselves better and feel more at home in their own lives. People often come to me for:

  • Anxiety, overthinking and constant worry
  • Low mood, burnout and feeling stuck
  • Grief and big life transitions
  • Relationship patterns that keep repeating
  • A harsh inner critic and low self-worth
  • Couples who keep having the same fight

You don’t need a crisis or a diagnosis to start counselling. Wanting things to feel different is enough.

Couples counselling in East Vancouver

I’m Gottman Level 1 trained, and couples work is one of my favourite parts of this job. Together we’ll look at how conflict actually plays out between you, build more everyday connection, and practise repair after the hard moments. Whether you’re dating, engaged, married or somewhere in between, the goal isn’t to decide who’s right. It’s to help you understand each other again.

How I work

My approach is person-centred and integrative, which means we follow what you need rather than a script. I’m curious about what happened to you, not just what’s wrong, and I see many of the things people struggle with as ways they learned to protect themselves.

I work from a feminist, anti-oppressive lens that takes your identity, culture and circumstances seriously. Having lived and worked in several countries before settling in Vancouver, I know there are many different ways people find healing, and I’ll respect yours.

Affordable counselling on Commercial Drive

Private counselling in Vancouver often costs well over $100 a session, which puts it out of reach for a lot of people. Because I’m completing my Master of Arts in Counselling Psychology at Yorkville University and working under clinical supervision, I offer a reduced intern rate, with a sliding scale if cost is a barrier.

Seeing a student counsellor can be a good fit for many people. You get someone who is well trained, closely supervised and genuinely invested in your progress, at a price that makes regular sessions possible.

Frequently asked questions

When can I start?
I begin seeing clients in January 2027. You can book a free consultation now to see if we’re a good fit, and we’ll find a start date that works for you.
How much does counselling cost?
I offer a reduced intern rate, with a sliding scale for people who need it. We can talk about what works for your budget during your free consultation.
Is counselling with a student counsellor covered by my benefits?
It depends on your plan. Many extended health plans only cover registered professionals, so it’s worth checking with your provider. Because my rate is reduced, many people find sessions affordable without coverage.
Do you see couples?
Yes. I work with couples of all kinds, in person on Commercial Drive and online across BC.
What happens in the free consultation?
It’s a short, relaxed conversation. You can tell me a little about what’s bringing you in, ask me anything about how I work, and see whether it feels like a good fit. There’s no pressure to book.
