About Hilary McKee, emerging psychotherapist in Vancouver, BC

Hi, I’m Hilary.

Whether you’re struggling in a relationship or struggling to feel like yourself, you deserve a space where all of you is welcome.

I’m completing my Master of Arts in Counselling Psychology, and from January 2027 I’ll see individuals and couples as a counselling intern, in person on Commercial Drive and online across BC. My approach is warm, collaborative and grounded in attachment, parts work and self-compassion, because I believe the patterns that keep you stuck once helped you survive. With care and curiosity, they can change.

I might be a good fit if you’re:

  • feeling anxious, low or burnt out, and not sure why
  • having the same argument with your partner over and over
  • anxious in relationships, or pulling away when things get close
  • caught in people-pleasing, perfectionism or a harsh inner critic
  • untangling what you learned growing up with emotionally distant or unpredictable parents
  • moving through a big life change or loss
  • simply wanting to understand yourself better

How I work

Collaborative, and at your pace.

I draw on attachment-based and relational approaches, Internal Family Systems (parts work), self-compassion practices, Gottman-informed couples work and body-aware, somatic tools.

In plain terms, we’ll get curious about the parts of you that learned to protect you, listen to what your body is telling you, and deepen the longest-lasting relationship you’ll ever have: the one with yourself.

What I believe

Your struggles don’t happen in a vacuum.

My approach is feminist and anti-oppressive. Culture, identity, family and the systems we live in all shape how we feel, and I’ll never ask you to leave any part of your story at the door.

Where it comes from

There are many ways to heal.

Years of travelling and living abroad in Asia, the Middle East and Europe taught me that, and that people know a great deal about what they need. I hold that with respect, and I’m curious about the traditions, communities and practices that matter to you.

If something here resonates, let’s talk.
